West High Soccer Camp

Two mornings of soccer over Thanksgiving break, coached by West High Varsity & JV players.

The West High Soccer Camp is a chance for young players in our community to build their soccer skills, have fun on the field and make new friends during the school break. Players of every experience level are welcome, from first-timers to young players who already play on a team.
